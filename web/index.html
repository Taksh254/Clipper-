    Good to know

    Scheduled shorts are uploaded right away with a publish time, so YouTube publishes them — your computer can be off.

    YouTube allows about 6 API uploads a day. Extra ones fail with a limit message; try them the next day.

    Until Google audits your API project, videos uploaded this way stay private, even when scheduled. Publish them from YouTube Studio → Content → Visibility until then.
